Ink · Sequential · Editorial
Rest

A surrealist comic exploring the emotional life we build within dreams and the unexplored aspects of the human condition they reveal. It reflects on how rest and dreaming uncover parts of ourselves that remain inaccessible in waking life.

PROJECT
Still Life · Digital Painting · Mixed Media
La Ofrenda

A still life study inspired by an ofrenda display at a Día de los Muertos celebration in Venice, hosted by SPARC. Wide brushstrokes left intentionally unblended evoke the soft edges of fading memory.

PROJECT
Landscape Design · Ecological Restoration · Illustration
Stone Canyon Creek

A native plant restoration design for land adjacent to UCLA's Anderson Building, translating ecological field research into a hand-annotated spatial plan using custom plant icons and companion complex theory.

PROJECT
Illustration · Storytelling
Thread by Thread

Blending impressionist textures and cubist-inspired character design, we explored the emotional journey of overcoming cancer. Through these visual techniques, we evoked feelings of resilience, anxiety, and perseverance for Pages for Pediatrics' mission to alleviate patient anxiety.

I'm Emily — a UCLA Biology grad who's spent the last four years pioneering storytelling from early ideas and concepts into finished visual products through illustration and design.

My background in biology and psychology taught me to pay attention to how humans think, feel, and connect. Through illustration and design, I create stories that carry both experiences and emotional resonance.

I hope to contribute my perspective and storytelling approach to teams and brands with meaningful ideas to share. If my work connects with your mission, I'd love to be part of the journey.
